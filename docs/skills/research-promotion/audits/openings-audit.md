# Audit of standfirsts and opening paragraphs

Recorded 2026-09-23 during development of research-promotion v1.1.0.
This record compares four accessible examples and distinguishes source
observations from proposed writing choices. It is retained for provenance
and maintenance; current instructions live in the [opening guide](../guidance/openings-and-language.md).

## What the references actually do

Read the standfirst as the short summary immediately below the headline. Count body
paragraphs from the first prose paragraph after it, excluding headings and captions.

| Reference and locator | Standfirst's job | Opening paragraphs' jobs | Language move to adapt |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Bocconi: Goodreads](https://www.unibocconi.it/en/news/chain-reaction-reshaped-goodreads), standfirst and body ¶1–3 | Expands a single policy change into several consequences | ¶1 establishes platforms' cultural role; ¶2 narrows to a dated fee change; ¶3 introduces the researchers and evidence | Broad subject becomes named actors doing specific things; a temporal turn carries the reader into the event |
| [Bocconi: framing questions](https://www.unibocconi.it/en/news/power-framing-questions), standfirst and body ¶1–4 | Exposes decision-shaping power in the ordering of questions | ¶1 invites an imagined restaurant choice; ¶2 explains the underlying power; ¶3–4 turn from helpful assistance to conflicting objectives | Second person makes an unfamiliar problem recognizable; ordinary choices introduce the abstraction |
| [Bocconi: X's algorithm](https://www.unibocconi.it/en/news/does-xs-algorithm-push-politics-right), standfirst and body ¶1–3 | Gives an exposure period and political consequence | ¶1 moves from a public concern to a gap in earlier evidence; ¶2 identifies the experiment; ¶3 gives a short answer | A question creates a precise evidence problem; a short verdict changes the pace after context |
| [The Economist via licensed Mint republication](https://www.livemint.com/economy/the-working-from-home-illusion-fades-11689140290522.html), body ¶1–5 | The displayed deck contrasts coordination costs with learning costs; the page also supplies a separate summary line | A workplace dispute leads to an apparent contradiction, a revised finding, then its explanation | Concrete workplaces precede economics vocabulary; surprise comes from changing evidence rather than adjectives |

Use each move where the source material supports it. The question-framing piece
is an author essay, so its first-person authority does not transfer to a university
reporter's voice. The other examples' superlatives and sweeping implications are
not instructions to raise the strength of a claim. See [source provenance](../references/sources.md)
for access limits; there is no verified NYT close reading in this collection.
