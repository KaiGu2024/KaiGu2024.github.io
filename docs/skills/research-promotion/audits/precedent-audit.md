# Audit of writing and agent-skill precedents

Recorded 2026-09-23 during initial skill development, with later pointers to the
opening and prose audits. These notes retain editorial interpretations and access
limits; they are not evidence of engagement effects or the current instruction set.
Use the [source catalog](../references/sources.md) for identities and provenance,
and `../guidance/` for current writing decisions. No external skill is required or
vendored. Scientific claims still require the underlying paper.

For close readings of the standfirsts and opening paragraphs, and instructions
derived from them, see [Openings and language](../guidance/openings-and-language.md). Its
word ranges and worked examples are editorial choices made for this skill, not
reported house rules from the referenced outlets.

For the systematic audit of words, sentences, length variation, paragraphs, and
pacing, see [Reference audit](prose-audit.md). It records observations and weaknesses
for seven accessible texts, with a feature-to-decision crosswalk. The operational
guidance is in [Prose editing](../guidance/prose-editing.md).

## Existing agent skills inspected

### Corey Haines: Social

[Primary skill](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/blob/main/skills/social/SKILL.md)
and [raw source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/main/skills/social/SKILL.md).
The repository currently names this skill `social`; directories still index an
older `social-content` name. The current primary source was read.

Useful ideas: identify audience and speaker, vary opening angles, and rebuild
source material for each channel. This research-specific skill adds paper-level
traceability and qualifications. It does not adopt the marketing skill's posting
frequencies, personal-story formulas, growth operations, or link-placement advice
as research communication rules.

### Social Media Skills: Thread Writer

[Primary skill](https://github.com/social-media-skills/skills/blob/main/skills/thread-writer/SKILL.md).
Full raw instruction text was accessible.

Useful ideas: one coherent argument across posts, a self-contained opening, and a
payoff; distinguish a post sequence from Meta's Threads app. The new skill avoids
requiring a brand-profile dependency or reshare/follow requests and adds research
design, evidence boundaries, and source attribution. This is an original synthesis,
not a copied skill or installation of the external package.

## University examples supplied by the user

### The Chain Reaction That Reshaped Goodreads

[Bocconi, Andrea Costa, 12 January 2026](https://www.unibocconi.it/en/news/chain-reaction-reshaped-goodreads).
Full article read.

The headline makes a policy change into an unfolding sequence. The opening frames
platforms as gatekeepers, then introduces a concrete fee change. The body follows
publishers, market composition, readers, and wider visibility; the ending returns
to access. Adapt the actor-by-actor progression when the paper supports it. Treat
the article's strong causal wording and superlatives as claims to check against
the paper, not ready-made language for another study.

### The Power of Framing Questions

[Bocconi, Martino Banchio, 25 May 2026](https://www.unibocconi.it/en/news/power-framing-questions).
Full article read; this is an author essay, unlike the staff-written examples.

An imagined restaurant recommendation makes a technical question about steering
choices recognizable. The article shifts from aligned to conflicting objectives,
introduces randomization through an analogy, and returns to the initial choice.
Adapt the scenario-to-mechanism-to-return structure for theory. Keep the scenario
hypothetical and make assumptions visible; do not convert model claims into
empirical findings.

### Does X's Algorithm Push Politics to the Right?

[Bocconi, Andrea Costa, 19 March 2026](https://www.unibocconi.it/en/news/does-xs-algorithm-push-politics-right).
Full article read.

A public-interest question leads into an experiment, followed by directional
results, outcomes that did not detectably change, an asymmetry, and a mechanism.
Subheads track reader questions. Adapt the separation between issue attitudes and
partisan identity. Its closing long-horizon question is rhetorical; a short-run
study does not establish years of cumulative effects.

## LinkedIn: directly accessible institutional posts

### Università Bocconi: visibility and scientific careers

[Original post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/universita-bocconi_visibility-success-sci-activity-7332796765315739648-qKVK).
Main post and institutional link comment were readable directly.

A broad question about professional success leads to a historical setting, named
researchers, a natural experiment, and differentiated career outcomes. Adapt the
movement from a widely relevant question to a specific credible comparison, and
retain who benefits versus who does not. Links in a comment are an observed
editorial choice, not evidence of a distribution advantage.

### Bocconi Marketing Department: perceptual disfluency and choice

[Original post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/bocconi-marketing-department_consumerbehavior-behavioralscience-marketingresearch-activity-7422195439182995456-5WO1).
Main post and DOI comment were readable directly.

Everyday examples of visual difficulty make an unfamiliar construct accessible.
The post identifies the researchers, scale of experimentation, result, and
conditions that strengthen or eliminate it before giving practical relevance.
Adapt the concrete-example-to-evidence progression and preserve boundary
conditions. The linked [paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/jcr/ucaf062) is the place to
verify research claims when writing about this result.

## X/Twitter: examples with an access limitation

Primary X pages returned no readable body. The following observations come from
posts displayed on a public [Eric Verhoogen profile mirror](https://mobile.twstalker.com/EricVerhoogen),
whose timestamp links exposed the primary status URLs. Mirror text is a provisional
style reference, not primary verification. Do not treat either as a fully read
thread, and recheck original text before quoting or attributing exact wording.

- [NBER post on marginal returns and misallocation](https://x.com/nberpubs/status/2018444359067172867):
  presents an economic interpretation, qualifies it with another explanation,
  credits authors, and links to a paper. Adapt one interpretive correction per
  short post, preserving the distinction between a review and original evidence.
- [Eric Verhoogen on quality upgrading in Colombian coffee](https://x.com/EricVerhoogen/status/2026691194902745530):
  points to a nontechnical summary, notes manuscript status, explains an incentive
  problem, and describes partial success. Adapt the plain-language mechanism and
  accurate status, rather than compressing partial improvement into a solution.

## Earned-media examples

### The Economist: The working-from-home illusion fades

[Licensed republication in Mint, 12 July 2023](https://www.livemint.com/economy/the-working-from-home-illusion-fades-11689140290522.html).
Full text accessible; the page identifies The Economist as author and explicitly
states republication under licence. Direct economist.com access was unavailable.

The article begins with a recognizable workplace dispute, pivots to a revision in
research evidence, compares studies, explains coordination and learning, then
introduces employee preferences. Adapt the question-to-evidence-to-tradeoff arc
and the habit of interrogating a convenient headline number. Do not import its
general conclusion into another study; remote and hybrid work, populations, and
research versions need to be distinguished. This is a historical style example,
not a current evidence review of remote work.

### Associated Press: productivity and automation

[Original report, 21 February 2024](https://apnews.com/article/inflation-pay-economy-productivity-workers-technology-prices-c1e0163d51b430fcb18f0c80e32b2d81).
Full article read. Search indexing and the current page use different headlines.

A documented factory problem opens into an economic puzzle; research and outside
voices explain it, followed by workers' experiences. Adapt the shift from concrete
stakes to evidence. The reporting is broader than one paper: its people, scenes,
and interviews cannot be manufactured from a manuscript-only brief.
