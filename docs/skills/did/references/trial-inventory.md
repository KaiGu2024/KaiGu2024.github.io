# AI-search case study: trial inventory

Evidence reviewed October 5, 2026. This is an inventory of distinct directions, not a new estimation exercise. Counts for different batches overlap and must not be added into a purported total number of independent tests. Historical notes sometimes refer to superseded figure numbers or shorter horizons; follow their dated numerical outputs, and use the current report/cache for current estimates.

The effort has been broad: measurement, sample composition, adoption and exposure definitions, comparison groups, weighting, transformations, counterfactual estimators, IV constructions, and other identifying variation have all been investigated. Several trials repaired measurement or improved balance. This is different from demonstrating a precise negative causal effect. Across the access designs, large negative estimates commonly retain trend concerns; stronger balance often weakens the decline or its precision. The self-adoption estimates address a different question and remain vulnerable to selection.

## Proposed, unavailable, or retired: do not claim completion

- **Full-panel question/navigation/long/short queries:** text availability was audited; representative query wording is unavailable. Generic routes and rare titles cannot substitute for it.
- **Verified eligibility, randomized rollout, or a conventional individual eligibility cutoff:** unavailable in these extracts. Frozen endpoint proxies do not by themselves supply exogeneity.
- **Full Freyaldenhoven–Hansen–Shapiro proxy identification:** not established. Auxiliary residualization was run; the required valid proxy/moments were not demonstrated.
- **Canonical SDID:** not the estimator used in the historical synthetic-weighted trials. In contrast, canonical fect algorithms were subsequently implemented and numerically checked.
- **Prior-year holiday counterfactuals:** proposed; earlier years were unavailable. Work-hour activity features were also unavailable in the relevant cache; weekday/weekend features were used.
- **Valid IV followed by ordinary adoption-timed CSDiD using thresholded fitted dates:** not produced. Related diagnostics were estimated and retired, not validated as that procedure.
- **Demeaned IV paths:** historical diagnostics, retired from the current design. No current identification claim relies on demeaning away leads.
- **February week25 or August follow-up:** unavailable as complete weekly outcomes; raw data end July31.

The remaining priorities are evidence about assignment, status/session observability, unaffected comparisons, and untreated seasonal behavior. Exhausting another small threshold grid cannot supply those facts.
