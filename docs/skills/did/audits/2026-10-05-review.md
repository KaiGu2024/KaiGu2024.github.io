# DiD adjustment skill review

Date: 2026-10-05. Verdict: suitable to sync as a methodological workflow; no blocking issue found in this review. Methodological instructions preserved; one external project link is adapted for portability.

## Scope

Reviewed the entrypoint, methodological modules, trial template, three evaluation scenarios, and scope statements of historical records. This is a document review and manual scenario walkthrough, not an independent agent benchmark or empirical replication. Historical estimates, software claims, and reported test pass counts were not independently reproduced.

Primary-source record/abstract checks support the cohort-time routing and aggregation discussion ([Callaway and Sant'Anna](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.09015)), panel versus repeated-cross-section doubly robust methods ([Sant'Anna and Zhao](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.01723)), and dedicated instrumented DiD routing ([Miyaji](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.12083)). These were targeted checks, not comprehensive citation or proof verification. The NBER stacked-DiD page could not be retrieved.

## Assessment

- Strong safeguards against favorable-sign searches, selective reporting, pretest acceptance as proof, and invented data.
- Explicit distinctions among assignment, uptake, population, outcome scale, horizon, and treatment history.
- Careful treatment of demeaning, pooled versus unit standardization, capping, trimming, operation order, and common-sample comparisons.
- Appropriate attention to full covariance, assignment-level dependence, survey design, few clusters, and weak-IV uncertainty.
- Actionable trial records and reproduction requirements. This is a reasoning workflow, not validated estimator software.

## Manual walkthroughs

These are reviewer-authored responses checking instruction coverage, not independently generated or blindly scored model outputs.

| Bundled scenario | Supported response |
|---|---|
| Firm energy mandate | Target the mandate effect in MWh; distinguish verified closures from missing observations; avoid automatic survivor-only selection. Diagnose measurement and TWFE contamination; use eligible cohort-time comparisons. Avoid single-noisy-baseline matching as a cure. Keep a raw-level benchmark; label logs/caps as different outcome questions. Reflect state assignment in inference. |
| Regional training IV | Payroll support is a concrete exclusion threat. Reject fitted adoption dates as a valid ordinary CSDiD IV procedure. Report grant-timed first stage and reduced form; distinguish the grant-package effect from a training effect. Preserve weak-IV uncertainty, including potentially unbounded sets, and the limitations of twelve assignment regions with three eligible. An insignificant reduced form proves neither no effect nor invalidity. |
| Repeated health surveys | Use repeated-cross-section methods; define the resident-population target and migration/composition issue. Do not fabricate person panels. Separate survey representation and causal weights. Avoid routine adjustment for treatment-affected income/employment. Define eligible district cohorts, anticipation/support, and inference compatible with district assignment and survey design. |

The written instructions cover the seven asserted decision elements in each scenario. This coverage assessment is not an empirical 21/21 benchmark score.

## Nonblocking improvements

1. Clarify the description's phrase 'preserving the estimand': preserve the original target as a benchmark and explicitly label alternatives. The body already correctly allows population and scale changes.
2. Expand evaluations to treatment reversals/carryover, common-date estimator equivalence, partial-period coverage, zero/small unit SDs, outcome-dependent trimming, covariance averaging, and significance-seeking requests. Add negative triggers for display-only edits.
3. Add numerical tests for fixed-demeaning invariance, pooled scaling, daily versus weekly caps, and full-covariance averages. These tests were not run here.
4. General procedures and historical evidence share the references directory. In a future restructuring, move procedures to guidance and completed trial records to audits, updating links together. The present sync preserves the original structure and methodological content.
5. The evaluation JSON mentions historical iteration-1 material that is not included in this portable bundle. Link a retained audit or clarify the external history; do not infer prior benchmark performance.
