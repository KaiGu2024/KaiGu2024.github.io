## Brynjolfsson, Li, and Raymond (2025)

**Publication:** Erik Brynjolfsson, Danielle Li, and Lindsey Raymond. *Generative AI at Work*. The Quarterly Journal of Economics, 140(2), 889–942. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjae044). [Inspected published full text](https://academic.oup.com/qje/article/140/2/889/7990658). [Crossref metadata](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1093/qje/qjae044). **VERIFIED.**

- **§VI.B, Worker Learning, first two paragraphs:** begins by asking whether earlier productivity gains represent durable learning or growing dependence on assistance, and explains the human-capital stakes. Only then does it introduce performance during system outages as evidence.
- **§VII.A, Customer Sentiment, opening three paragraphs:** establishes difficult customer interactions and competing ways AI could affect them, then introduces sentiment measurement and subsequently the estimates.

## Egger et al. (2022)

**Publication:** Dennis Egger, Johannes Haushofer, Edward Miguel, Paul Niehaus, and Michael Walker. *General Equilibrium Effects of Cash Transfers: Experimental Evidence From Kenya*. Econometrica, 90(6), 2603–2643. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3982/ECTA17945). [Inspected published PDF](https://www.givedirectly.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/General-equilibrium-effects-of-cash-transfers-experimental-evidence-from-Kenya.pdf). [Crossref metadata](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.3982/ECTA17945). **VERIFIED.**

- **§4.5, Effects on Output Prices, p. 2626, opening paragraph:** the first sentence explains that price responses determine whether preceding monetary impacts represent real gains. The paragraph then reports price findings. One sentence provides the local purpose; no repeated methods exposition is needed.

## Gordon, Moakler, and Zettelmeyer (2023)

**Publication:** Brett R. Gordon, Robert Moakler, and Florian Zettelmeyer. *Close Enough? A Large-Scale Exploration of Non-Experimental Approaches to Advertising Measurement*. Marketing Science, 42(4), 768–793. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2022.1413). [Crossref metadata](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1287/mksc.2022.1413). **VERIFIED.**

**Inspected version:** [arXiv v2 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.07055v2), dated September 21, 2022. The citation year is the journal publication year; passage wording and printed page numbers refer to this manuscript, not a verified copy of the final journal text.

- **§5.4, Reducing Selection Bias, pp. 32–33:** opens with the question raised by poor observational performance: do adjustments at least improve on the unadjusted comparison? It explains what failure would imply about specifications or features before presenting comparative evidence.
- **Boundary case, §5.3, Results, p. 28:** starts with a procedural description of statistical comparisons rather than a fresh statement of local stakes. The manuscript therefore does not support a claim that all Results openings follow the preferred sequence.
