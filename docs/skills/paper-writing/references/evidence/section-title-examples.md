## Editorial sources and scope

- [APA: Headings](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/paper-format/headings) supports concise, descriptive headings and a hierarchy scaled to the document.
- [MLA: Parallel Headings in Research Papers](https://style.mla.org/parallel-headings/) discusses parallel grammar and comparable heading length.
- [MIT/Broad: Results](https://mitcommlab.mit.edu/broad/commkit/journal-article-results/) and [AIP: Research Manuscript](https://publishing.aip.org/resources/researchers/publishing-academy/preparing-for-submission/research-manuscript/) offer conclusion-based results headings as an approach. AIP also uses technique-based methods headings.
- [AMA submission guidelines](https://www.ama.org/submission-guidelines-american-marketing-association-journals/) supply venue-specific heading formats; consult current requirements for submission.

The emphasis on substantive questions and the continuous Discussion section are the author's chosen drafting defaults. The selected papers illustrate available forms; they do not establish how frequently journals use them or require adopting a paper's full structure.
