# Academic-voice audit: three recognized empirical papers

Audited 2026-09-22 and extended systematically on 2026-09-23 to refine [academic-voice.md](../academic-voice.md). These are the same three papers used in the [introduction audit](introduction-three-paper-audit.md), which records verified bibliographic metadata, recognition, exact versions, introduction paragraph labels, and PDF checksums. This follow-up examines prose choices rather than prescribing paragraph order. The recommendations are editorial inferences, not journal frequencies or evidence that these choices caused publication success. The [writing-source review](academic-voice-sources.md) distinguishes published advice from empirical evidence and our synthesis.

## Observations and resulting edits

The introduction labels E, M, and I refer to the earlier audit. Summaries below paraphrase the passages; they are not model sentences to reuse in unrelated projects.

| Prose feature | Evidence in the sampled passages | Implication for the skill |
|---|---|---|
| Subjects reflect the claim's role | E3–E4 describe author actions; E6–E8 shift to enterprises, households, and prices. M8 alternates the authors' finding with the methods' performance. I5 alternates platform actions and author findings. | Preserve that functional variation. The author's impersonal-subject preference should not become a rule against “we.” |
| Confidence changes with the inference | E6 and E12 distinguish patterns from an explicitly speculative capacity explanation. M11 and the heterogeneity discussion on p. 38 mark author interpretations as conjectures. I6 states findings; §6 distinguishes them from unresolved long-term consequences. | State supported results directly and qualify explanations locally. Avoid both blanket hedging and an unmarked jump from result to mechanism. |
| Syntax makes comparisons legible | M8 grants one method's relative improvement before stressing its remaining error. I5 places hiring benefits beside disintermediation costs. E8 separates statistical precision from economic magnitude. | Keep the relevant comparator and concession close to the finding. Use parallel clauses where they reduce the burden of comparison. |
| Technical clarity does not require uniform formality | M3 explains the ad-delivery process before its identification problem; M6 supplies an intuitive account of an assumption. I4 explains trust through services users need less. E12 illustrates a production constraint concretely. | Explain the unfamiliar link while retaining precise terms. Neither extra notation nor exclusively abstract nouns are prerequisites for academic voice. |
| Evidence supports an interpretive step | Egger §4.1, p. 2618, compares expenditure estimates and explains their relevance to spillovers. Gu and Zhu §4.1, p. 7, turn the hiring/revenue contrast into a question for §4.2. Gordon §5.3, p. 31, explains why a relative-error measure needs a complementary absolute-error measure. | Make the reader's next inference explicit. A locator helps inspect evidence; a list of columns alone does not explain its significance. |
| Repetition changes function across sections | The introductions preview central findings; the sampled results attach evidence; the conclusions revisit what those findings establish. Their repeated terms retain recognizable objects of comparison. | Preserve deliberate continuity while removing repetition that serves no additional purpose. Do not pursue compression through synonym substitution or deletion of every repeated finding. |
| Limits specify what cannot be inferred | Egger's conclusion discusses conditions affecting transportability. Gu and Zhu's §6 distinguishes observed short-term effects from opposing longer-term possibilities. Gordon §7.1 treats better data infrastructure as a prospective route needing further evidence. | Make a limitation govern a particular inference or decision. Retain the distinction between an empirical result and a proposed remedy. |

## What not to imitate mechanically

- **First person and tense:** all three use first person, but three papers do not establish venue-wide preferences. Tense varies with author actions, empirical summaries, and general relationships; do not standardize it by journal label.
- **Table narration:** Gordon §5.3 and Gu and Zhu §4.2 include substantial display-oriented exposition. Orientation can help, but the skill should retain its preference for a substantive comparison and decisive evidence over a column tour.
- **Numerical phrasing:** Gordon's p. 31 moves among ratios, percentage error, and multiplicative language; Gu and Zhu's p. 7 uses percent notation around changes in hiring shares. These are reasons to verify denominators and units, not wording conventions to adopt. This audit does not adjudicate or revise their estimates.
- **Strength of wording:** phrases claiming confirmation, broad novelty, or absence of an effect need their own evidentiary support. Recognition of a paper does not make every such formulation transferable. A voice-only edit must flag questionable inference rather than silently strengthen or weaken it.

The audit supports practical choices about agency, comparisons, explanation, and confidence. It does not justify new phrase blacklists, sentence-length quotas, a mandatory question in every results section, or separate house-style profiles from one paper per venue.

## Systematic extension: words, sentences, paragraphs, and pacing

### Protocol and coverage

We reread **all 43 introduction paragraphs**, checking each for (1) lexical precision and abstraction, (2) agency and verb clarity, (3) information order and emphasis, (4) sentence structure and length variation, (5) paragraph job and development, (6) cohesion and pacing, and (7) evidence, attribution, and qualification. The ledger below records each paragraph's length pattern and a salient observation, rather than assigning subjective quality scores. The dimension matrix then compares the papers and records qualifications to the proposed principles. Results/conclusion evidence in the earlier table remains a selected qualitative cross-check; the numerical census covers introductions only.

Paragraph boundaries were checked against rendered pages, including continuations across pages and columns. Body prose excludes abstracts, headings, running headers, footnotes, and superscript note markers. Inline citations, numbers, and parenthetical explanations remain because readers encounter them in the sentence. We normalized PDF ligatures, joined wrapped words, and inspected sentence segmentation for decimals, abbreviations, citations, and question marks inside an example list. A colon or semicolon does not create a new sentence. Word counts use whitespace-delimited tokens containing a letter or number; hyphenation and citation conventions affect counts. These are descriptive token counts, not a measure of conceptual difficulty. We did not count printed rows, score readability, or treat subjective “messages” as mechanically countable units.

### Comparison by principle and decision

| Dimension | Evidence and counterexample | Guidance decision |
|---|---|---|
| Vocabulary and abstraction | E12 and M3 connect abstract mechanisms to concrete processes; I1 supplies instances of intermediation. M5–M6 retain precise method names, and I5 defines its measure. | Combine technical precision with explanation of the unfamiliar link. Avoid both jargon inflation and forced simplification. |
| Agency and action | E3–E4 foreground author actions, M3 foregrounds platforms, and I4 foregrounds users. Established nouns such as trust and disintermediation carry the argument across sentences. | Prefer recoverable actors/actions, while retaining abstract nouns that name the actual constructs. No ban on nominalization or first person. |
| Information order and emphasis | E1's closing gap prepares the study; I1's closing risk becomes I2's opening topic. M8 places the decisive qualification in a separate brief sentence. | Check what the main clause and closure emphasize. Topic continuity can justify repeated words and openings. |
| Length and syntactic burden | E4 and M6 use parallel lists to organize long sentences. M11's final sentence combines extrapolation, caveat, and comparison across 75 tokens. I4 works with lengths 27, 29, 27. | Inspect dependency burden and competing emphases. The longest sentence is not automatically a model; similar lengths are not automatically a defect. |
| Paragraph unity and completion | E5 and I9 are complete two-sentence paragraphs. M4 has eight sentences serving one empirical overview. E6 adds a methodological implication to substantive findings; M15 combines historical positioning and direct comparison. | One governing job may contain multiple claims. Split broader paragraphs if their implications need independent development, without splitting merely to hit a count. |
| Pacing across paragraphs | E5–E9 develop recipients, firms, non-recipients, prices, then aggregation. M8–M11 proceed from performance through qualifications to diagnosis. I7–I9 build literature, shortcoming, then contribution. | Allocate space to inferential steps and make handoffs meaningful. Do not prescribe equal blocks, one paragraph per finding, or alternating long/short paragraphs. |
| Evidence, attribution, and qualification | E2 and I7 have substantial citation clusters. M11 marks author conjecture; I9 keeps the disintermediation condition attached to its contribution. E10 ends with a data limitation. | Attribute claims accurately and qualify locally. A citation-heavy sentence may need restructuring, but removing necessary support is not a voice improvement. |
| Endings and signposting | E1 ends in a gap, E10 in a limitation, M13 in an implication, and M19/I12 in navigation. E12 ends tentatively and has no roadmap. | Match closure to function. Neither an emphatic takeaway nor a mechanical restatement belongs at every paragraph ending; retain the author's requested introduction roadmap as a separate drafting preference. |

This check supports functional flexibility and identifies useful diagnostic questions. It does not validate causal benefits of these techniques, ideal length ranges, or every sentence in the exemplars. Guidance on punctuation, abbreviation economy, and citation scope also draws on editorial synthesis in the source note; the three-paper census did not separately estimate their frequencies or effects.
