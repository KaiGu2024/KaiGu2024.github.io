## Observed development

**Economics.** A separate Discussion uses the joint output, input, and price patterns to develop a capacity-utilization interpretation. It distinguishes indirect evidence from unmeasured utilization, connects the explanation to literature, and labels deeper speculation. The Conclusion synthesizes findings, develops implications for evaluating transfers and aggregate effects, and considers generalization through targeting, business conditions, geography, and financing. Its final paragraph contrasts theories about persistence and motivates long-run follow-up. Interpretation, external validity, and future research receive sustained reasoning rather than a fixed one-sentence treatment. [Source](https://www.givedirectly.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/General-equilibrium-effects-of-cash-transfers-experimental-evidence-from-Kenya.pdf)

**Marketing.** The Conclusion revisits three contributions before developing paths for researchers and industry: richer logging, existing quasi-random variation, injected randomness, and closer attention to decisions. The paths include feasibility, engineering, and target-effect considerations. The closing distinction is that inaccurate effect estimates need not produce different decisions. This extends the empirical comparison into a question about its practical use; it does not claim the paper tested all proposed solutions. The structure supports an agenda organized by substantive alternatives rather than a mandatory paragraph for each audience. [Source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.07055v2)

**Information systems.** The combined closing section moves from the central finding to unobserved forms of disintermediation, the short horizon, and an unresolved causal relationship involving repeated interaction. Opposing long-run forces leave the net effect ambiguous. The authors distinguish their finding from an optimal-policy or net-benefit analysis, then discuss possible platform responses using outside examples. The final sentence calls for testing those strategies. Limitations therefore help determine the practical inference; they do not simply follow implications in a standard order. [Source](http://fengzhu.info/disintermediation.pdf)

## Changes to the guide

| Prior gap or rigid prescription | Revision |
|---|---|
| A claimed recurring four-part sequence | Offer combined sections, separate interpretation and conclusion, or a compact conclusion; choose by argumentative work. |
| One sentence to restate the result | Synthesize the relationship among findings at the length needed to answer the question. |
| Little guidance on relating the answer back to theory | Explain what the evidence changes in a literature-based relationship; allow modest theoretical contribution. |
| Stakeholder paragraphs treated as the default | Organize implications by real decisions and distinct incentives; include relevant audiences only. |
| Practical advice lacked a bridge from evidence to action | Identify objectives, conditions, trade-offs, and the difference between an observed effect and an optimal choice. |
| Limitations treated chiefly as scope or data constraints | Distinguish scope from inferential threats and explain their consequences without rhetorical reframing. |
| A dominant threat plus a prescribed next study | Develop consequential open questions and discriminating evidence; allow a focused agenda or no separate future-research list. |
| An unsupported two-page ceiling and required report input | Remove both; retain useful closure guidance and flexible links to shared drafting principles. |

## Evidentiary limits and skill-creator review

These are selected examples, not a representative sample or evidence that one structure causes publication success. All use randomized evidence in some role. Their organizational choices vary, and none establishes a universal order, paragraph count, or page limit. The studies' historical platform examples are inspected as writing moves, not reverified as current business practices.

The revised guidance combines those observations with editorial judgment. In particular, scaling theoretical claims to the contribution, distinguishing scope from threats, and placing substantial new empirical analyses with adequate methods and evidence are general writing decisions, not conventions demonstrated by every exemplar. A specific significance close is the retained author-facing preference; the sources show that a well-motivated open question can also perform that function.

The skill-creator pass keeps reusable guidance separate from this audit and removes fixed counts, required artifacts, defensive audience checklists, and unsupported claims about all award papers. Introduction retains the contribution preview, Conceptual Framework grounds constructs and relationships, and Results develops evidence and local interpretations. Discussion uses those foundations to explain the answer's broader meaning and limits. Shared voice and evidence-preservation rules are linked instead of repeated as a second audit procedure.
