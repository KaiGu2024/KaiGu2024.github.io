## Observed argumentative development

**Economics.** Egger et al. organize evidence around spending moving from recipients to enterprises and nonrecipients. Price evidence then informs whether monetary gains represent real activity. The multiplier section combines components into an aggregate quantity, explaining coverage and accounting choices. Uncertainty is tied to substantive thresholds; precise price estimates constrain inflation interpretations, while multiplier tests distinguish positivity from exceeding one. Comparisons and alternative assumptions appear beside the inference they qualify. Some interpretations remain tentative, and disagreement with earlier price estimates is left for future research. [Source](https://www.givedirectly.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/General-equilibrium-effects-of-cash-transfers-experimental-evidence-from-Kenya.pdf)

**Marketing.** Gordon et al. establish experimental effects before assessing observational methods. They combine visual distributions with numerical summaries and supplement relative errors with absolute errors because denominators affect interpretation. Their next question distinguishes improvement over an unadjusted comparison from accuracy against experimental estimates. Variation in performance motivates a further analysis of campaign characteristics, with explanatory conjectures identified as such. A footnote separates the median of differences from differences of medians. The progression illustrates connected evaluation criteria rather than a list of methods that performed best. [Source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.07055v2)

**Information systems.** Gu and Zhu connect hiring patterns and platform revenue to a puzzle, then use transaction outcomes and message evidence to examine disintermediation. Heterogeneity follows reasoning about when the behavior would be more likely. The robustness section addresses distinct concerns, including contamination, worker selection, ambiguous message terms, and client satisfaction. A revenue calculation makes the proposed offset economically interpretable. The section illustrates complementary measures and concern-led follow-up analysis; it does not establish that every empirical paper needs a separate mechanism section. [Source](http://fengzhu.info/disintermediation.pdf)

## Revisions and their basis

| Existing gap or rigid instruction | Revised guidance |
|---|---|
| Connected findings coexisted with a mandatory roadmap and artifact sequence | Organize by reader dependencies; use orientation when useful and allow findings and interpretation to span paragraphs. |
| Headline estimates dominated the presentation advice | Explain joint patterns, offsetting responses, aggregation, and the distinct questions answered by different benchmarks. |
| Numerical comparisons lacked enough scale guidance | Clarify denominators, absolute versus relative measures, aggregation, and comparability across studies. |
| Mechanisms required two to three prespecified tests | Make mechanism development conditional; accurately distinguish planned and exploratory work and levels of evidentiary support. |
| Heterogeneity guidance mainly required theory-motivated cuts | Explain the substantive contrast, group composition, and evidence for differences; allow clearly exploratory analysis. |
| Nulls were automatically attributed to power or uncertainty | Distinguish precise small effects from imprecision and interpret meaningful thresholds. |
| Robustness prescribed one table and exhaustive work | Organize checks by concerns, retain material sensitivity in the main text, and defer repetitive details. |
| Each interpretation required a quotable verdict | Use synthesis when it adds understanding; let qualified or unresolved conclusions stand. |

These choices combine observed exposition with editorial and statistical judgment. Difference tests, multiplicity reporting, and the distinction between a sequence of effects and an identified mechanism are general safeguards for interpreting results, not practices established across all three papers. The structural extension retains the guide's broader scope; these exemplars do not validate a universal structural-paper order.

## Limits and skill-creator review

The papers are selected exemplars, not a representative sample of journal practice. All use randomized evidence in some role. Their prose is useful evidence of organization, but strong source wording is not automatically suitable guidance: insignificant differences do not prove equivalence, proxies do not become direct observations, and follow-up checks do not conclusively eliminate every alternative. The guide preserves these distinctions without requiring a methodological audit during routine drafting.

The revision removes fixed mechanism and robustness counts, compulsory report artifacts, a universal structural sequence, manuscript-specific hypotheticals, and unsupported claims about display frequencies. It retains useful existing guidance on accounting identities, scenario construction, uncertainty displays, and consistent repeated magnitudes in more general language. Shared voice and construct rules remain in their existing guides; local methods point to Data and Methods, while broader implications point to Discussion. No new paragraph quota, approval step, or routine reporting obligation is added. This audit supplies provenance; the current section guide governs drafting.
