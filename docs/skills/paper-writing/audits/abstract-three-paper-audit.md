# Abstract audit: three established empirical papers

Audited 2026-09-23 against [abstract.md](../references/sections/abstract.md) at repository revision `160b10d`. This record explains the resulting edits. It compares the same three recognized empirical papers as the [introduction audit](../references/evidence/introduction-three-paper-audit.md), which records recognition and bibliographic verification. Observations are distinguished from editorial recommendations; three selected papers cannot establish journal-wide conventions or show that a writing choice caused publication success.

**Author calibration, 2026-09-23:** retain connected findings, flexible detail, and standalone comprehension. Give data or methodological advantages space only when central to the contribution, without making answerability a separate promotional task. Require a closing significance sentence. The varied endings observed below remain evidence about the papers; they do not override this drafting preference. The editorial decisions below incorporate this calibration.

## How the abstracts develop

| Paper | Observed progression |
|---|---|
| Egger et al. | E1 combines the economic question with an experimental-evidence gap. E2–E3 give the intervention, randomized setting, and economically meaningful scale. E4–E6 connect recipient outcomes, spillovers, prices, and a multiplier. E7 previews a welfare interpretation framework rather than stating a separate policy prescription. |
| Gordon et al. | M1–M4 move from an unavailable measurement option to the selection problem its substitute must solve. M5–M7 establish an experimental benchmark, unusually rich observational information, and competing methods. M8–M10 distinguish relative method performance from accuracy against the benchmark. M11 previews heterogeneity; M12 returns to the unresolved measurement task. |
| Gu and Zhu | I1 explains a construct relationship and its tension; I2 connects the question to a randomized marketplace study. I3–I4 develop a benefit, a condition-dependent offsetting cost, and their revenue consequence. I5 previews variation across participants. I6 points toward platform responses without reporting a test of those responses. |

These progressions support the existing functional approach. They do not support equal space for each function, a mandatory theoretical opening, one result per sentence, or a fixed numerical quota.

## What the guide covered and what was missing

| Dimension | Assessment before revision | Editorial decision |
|---|---|---|
| Construct, relationship, and problematization | Already well covered. I1 demonstrates compressed conceptual framing; E1 and M1–M4 offer other legitimate openings. | Retain the conceptual option without requiring abstract definitions or a theoretical dispute. An empirical-evidence gap or practical inferential obstacle can motivate the study. |
| Why this study can answer | Setting and design were present, but their connection to the obstacle was implicit. E1–E3 and M4–M7 make that connection informative. | Keep the empirical-object function focused on understanding the evidence. Following the author's calibration, mention a data or methodological advantage only when central to the contribution; do not routinely build a separate argument for unique answerability. |
| Research question and answer alignment | The guide named the problem and answer separately. M12 makes their connection particularly visible. | Read opening and ending together: the findings should answer the question actually posed, at the supported scope. No separate question sentence is required. |
| Multiple findings | A singular central-finding move and a pattern–number–interpretation formula understated the need to connect several findings. | Write the relationship among findings, including changes in level, condition, or comparator. A logical progression is broader than an accounting decomposition. |
| Numbers and method detail | Existing exceptions were helpful, but a quantitative anchor could sound obligatory. I1–I6 contain no numerical estimates; M7–M10 devote substantial space to methods and matched benchmarks. | Allow qualitative answers and multiple meaningful estimates. Allocate detail by the contribution rather than applying a universal number or method-name limit. |
| Significance and ending | E7, M12, and I6 perform different closing jobs; the initial audit inferred that a separate significance sentence could be optional. | The author explicitly chooses a closing significance sentence. State the supported consequence for understanding or action, connecting the findings to the opening problem. This replaces the initial optional-closing recommendation without changing the source observations. |
| Previewing secondary work | The evidence hierarchy appropriately favors findings, but could be read as banning every announcement of an analysis. E7, M11, and I5–I6 preview secondary work. | A useful secondary preview can support the interpretation, but does not replace the core answer or closing significance statement. Where supported and affordable, naming its conclusion is more informative than merely announcing it. |
| Abstract self-sufficiency | Construct distinctions were present, but standalone comprehension was not explicit. I1 briefly explains its less familiar central concept. | Supply the conceptual or comparison detail needed to understand the answer without importing the introduction's literature review or roadmap. |
| Decomposition emphasis | A substantial standalone example gave one conditional pattern unusual prominence. The three abstracts also use broader sequences of findings. | Retain the accounting safeguards as conditional guidance, remove the project-shaped hypothetical, and expand the central-finding function to cover other logical relationships. |

## Limits and choices not to copy

The observed sentence counts describe these versions, not recommended lengths. Current venue limits require separate verification when drafting for submission. Publisher HTML paragraph breaks are not evidence of a required manuscript paragraph format.

Methodological detail can be essential, but dense acronym and numerical sequences still impose reading costs. Recognition does not make every sentence an optimal model. Generic previews can be legitimate without being maximally informative. None of these examples supplies a universal opening, closing, or ideal balance of setup and results.

All three use randomized evidence in some role. The guide's treatment of purely descriptive, observational, qualitative, or structural-model contributions is therefore not independently validated by this set. Nor does this comparison validate rules for citations, structured abstracts, or mandatory limitation sentences. Retain the existing evidence-preservation guidance without inventing venue rules from these omissions.

The revision preserves the five functions and evidence hierarchy, adds the missing decision criteria, and removes the rigid summary formula and narrow hypothetical. The current section guide governs drafting; this record supplies provenance and critique.
