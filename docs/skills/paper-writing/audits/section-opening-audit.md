# Section-opening audit

Date: 2026-11-01. Scope: the shared main-text guidance and Results guide, checked against selected passages in three empirical papers. [Source records and locators](../references/evidence/section-opening-examples.md) contain the publication metadata, inspected versions, and verification results.

## Gap in the previous guidance

The Results guide already called for question-based organization, orientation to comparisons, motivated heterogeneity, and concern-led sensitivity checks. It also advised bringing findings forward when comparisons were familiar. It did not explicitly require the opening of each substantive section or subsection to establish both its local question and why the answer matters before procedures or findings.

## Observations and decision

The inspected examples show three useful opening functions: distinguish competing interpretations of preceding findings, explain why a new outcome changes their meaning, and motivate a diagnostic comparison after a disappointing result. Their setup ranges from a single sentence to several paragraphs. One inspected Results opening instead starts procedurally, so these papers cannot establish a universal journal convention or a measured benefit of the preferred style.

Adopt the user's requested preference as a house drafting rule: establish the local question and its stakes before technique or finding. Extend it to substantive sections throughout the main text as an editorial decision, with particularly explicit instructions for Results. The small, purposive sample illustrates this rule; it does not independently validate its application to every section type.

## Integration and checks

- Put the shared rule in main-text and a brief pointer in SKILL.md; put the empirical sequence and teaching example in Results.
- Reconcile the existing finding-forward advice: after the purpose is established, move promptly to the finding; later paragraphs need not restart the motivation.
- Allow a preceding transition to supply the setup and an umbrella heading to lead directly to its subsections. Keep abstracts under their own guide.
- Check openings for both question and stakes, proportional length, faithful planned/exploratory status, and preservation of evidence. A generic announcement of an analysis does not satisfy the rule.

This was a source and instruction-consistency review, not a benchmark of generated manuscripts or a systematic review of disciplinary practice.
