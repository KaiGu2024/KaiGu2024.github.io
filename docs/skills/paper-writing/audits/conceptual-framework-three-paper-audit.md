# Conceptual-framework audit: three established empirical papers

Audited 2026-09-23 against [conceptual-framework.md](../references/sections/conceptual-framework.md) at repository revision `e8127d5`. This extends the initial purpose-and-boundaries guide using the same three empirical papers as the [introduction audit](../references/evidence/introduction-three-paper-audit.md), which records their recognition and bibliographic verification. The author's starting point is retained: relevant literatures help readers understand and position the constructs and theoretical relationships. This audit examines how that foundation develops into reasoning useful for the empirical investigation.

**Author calibration after revision `f9f6137`, 2026-09-23:** for substantive empirical papers with modest theoretical contributions, introducing the major constructs and their relationships through existing literature is the section's primary work. The first revision gave extended explanatory development too much default weight. The current guide therefore centers conceptual grounding and synthesis, followed by the study's adopted perspective and connection to its question. Mechanisms, competing predictions, and formal models receive fuller development only when the argument needs them. This calibrates the editorial decisions below without changing observations about the papers.

## Observed development

### Economics: a framework for interpretation

Egger et al. place their explicit welfare framework after the multiplier estimates. Section 6 begins by separating output from welfare and relating macroeconomic and program-evaluation perspectives. It defines a welfare-equivalent transfer, introduces a household optimization problem, and distinguishes changes in feasible choices from external effects of others' behavior. Sections 6.1–6.2 then connect those distinctions to evidence. Increased production requires different welfare interpretations depending on productivity gains and input opportunity costs. Section 7 develops a capacity-utilization explanation while acknowledging that utilization was not directly measured. Earlier, §4.1 explicitly leaves the earnings response theoretically ambiguous. These passages illustrate conceptual distinctions, conditional reasoning, and interpretation without requiring an early hypothesis section. [Source](https://www.givedirectly.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/General-equilibrium-effects-of-cash-transfers-experimental-evidence-from-Kenya.pdf)

### Marketing: institutional processes clarify an inferential problem

Gordon et al. develop the relevant account through platform background and methods. Sections 2.1–2.3 distinguish eligibility, assignment, and exposure, explaining how user activity, auctions, and changing predictions generate selection. Section 5 begins with a hypothetical campaign without an experimental control group. It uses that situation to motivate the observational comparison and then states the assumptions required for adjustment. The discussion of overlap connects an abstract condition to concrete sources of exposure variation. This is conceptual clarification of a measurement problem, not a freestanding behavioral theory. Its transferable writing lesson is to explain the process that makes a distinction or assumption consequential, rather than insert technical definitions without a role in the argument. [Source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.07055v2)

### Information systems: a relationship developed through competing consequences

Gu and Zhu's introduction connects trust to both successful exchange and reduced reliance on intermediary services. It uses reputation and disintermediation literatures to develop that tension. Section 2 explains the transaction process, fees, and reputation information that make the reasoning concrete. Section 4.3 supplies local arguments for variation across conditions, including proximity and job divisibility. Section 5.2 considers whether freelancer selection could explain lower charges and hours, connecting alternative explanations to additional observations. Section 6 distinguishes short-run responses from unresolved longer-run effects. These functions are distributed across the paper; their placement does not establish that every argument was formulated before analysis. [Source](http://fengzhu.info/disintermediation.pdf)

## Gaps in the initial guide and resulting decisions

The initial guide already distinguished constructs, theory, literature synthesis, optional predictions, and section boundaries. The missing detail concerned how to turn those principles into an account readers can follow.

| Gap | Editorial decision for the section guide |
|---|---|
| Literature positioning could remain descriptive | Explain what each perspective accounts for, its assumptions, and how the study adopts or combines it. Organize around relationships and conceptual questions. |
| Definitions lacked actor, level, and temporal scope | Specify whose construct or outcome is involved; explain movement across levels and consequential time horizons. Keep concepts distinct from interventions and indicators. |
| The move from relationship to explanation was implicit | Explain the literature's reasoning as part of the core synthesis. Following author calibration, make extended mechanism and moderator development conditional on the argument's needs. |
| Competing channels had little development guidance | When competing channels matter, explain how they interact and whether assumptions determine a net prediction. Preserve indeterminacy; do not require competing accounts in every study. |
| The empirical handoff was underspecified | Connect the adopted concepts and relationships to what the study examines. Derive discriminating observations when relevant, without making theory discrimination or methodological superiority a default task. |
| Section form and placement needed decision criteria | Allow integrated reasoning, a separate early section, or a later interpretive framework. Select verbal development, predictions, or formalization according to their argumentative use. |
| Prediction and interpretation needed a clearer boundary | Distinguish advance expectations, empirical findings, and explanations developed in interpreting them; do not infer research chronology from exposition order. |

These decisions synthesize observed practices with the author's goals. They are guidance for drafting a coherent account, not claims that each paper explicitly articulates every principle. The empirical handoff is about relevance to the question, not a requirement to stress data or methodological advantages.

## Limits and implementation review

This set contains no direct exemplar of a dedicated early conceptual-framework section with a full sequence of numbered hypotheses. It therefore does not validate a universal paragraph order, section length, hypothesis format, or level of formalization. A task requiring close calibration to that particular format would benefit from an additional directly comparable exemplar. The current guide can still develop the underlying functions without pretending the three papers share that structure.

All three use randomized evidence in some role. Their practices do not independently validate guidance specific to inductive qualitative theory building or purely observational research. Recognized publication quality is not evidence that every expository choice should be copied. The comparison assesses writing functions, not the validity of every theoretical or causal claim in the papers.

The skill-creator review keeps the resulting guide focused on section development: source observations remain here; shared construct and voice rules are linked; figures, formal models, hypotheses, and separate sections remain conditional. No new approval gate, routine audit deliverable, or manuscript-specific hypothetical is introduced. The current [section guide](../references/sections/conceptual-framework.md) governs drafting; this record explains its basis and limits.
