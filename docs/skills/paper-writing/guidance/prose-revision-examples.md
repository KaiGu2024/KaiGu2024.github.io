# Prose revision examples

Use these examples when applying the [academic-voice guidance](../references/academic-voice.md). They are invented teaching passages. Their facts illustrate editing choices and supply no evidence about a user's paper.

## Give an overloaded sentence room

**Before:**

> Entry growth alongside declining average usage and rising numbers of heavily used products suggests an expansion in the supply of products that attract substantial audiences despite lower usage per product.

**After:**

> The number of products entering the market rose. Average usage per product fell. Even so, the number of products attracting substantial audiences rose.

Each sentence adds one part of the comparison. "Even so" connects the falling average to the rise in products with substantial audiences. A full Results passage would also retain the supplied magnitudes and definition of a substantial audience.

## Give a new argumentative task its own paragraph

**Before:**

> New sellers entered the platform after fees fell. Entry rose most in categories that had previously attracted few sellers. Lower fees may have made entry affordable for sellers with limited expected sales. The larger increase in these categories is consistent with that explanation.

**After:**

> New sellers entered the platform after fees fell. Entry rose most in categories that had previously attracted few sellers.
>
> Lower fees offer one possible explanation for entry into these categories. They may have made entry affordable for sellers with limited expected sales.

The first paragraph establishes the pattern. The second develops a possible explanation and retains its uncertainty. Its opening connects the explanation to the evidence already presented. The added space gives the interpretation its own step.

## Show why the next comparison matters

**Context:** the preceding paragraph documents growth in the number of products.

**Before:**

> Furthermore, we examine usage. Table 3 reports usage of the new products.

**After:**

> To understand what this expansion means for consumers, we next examine how much the new products are used. Table 3 reports their usage.

The opening connects product growth to consumer use and gives the next analysis a purpose. The subsequent evidence must determine what can be concluded about consumers. Use such orientation where the connection needs explaining; a paragraph with an already clear connection can begin with its substantive point.

## State the measure directly

**Context:** this sentence introduces a measure of publicly declared policies; actual behavior is outside this paragraph's question.

**Before:**

> The measure captures declared policies, not actual behavior.

**After:**

> The measure records the policies firms publicly declare.

The revised sentence defines the measured object directly. If an analysis depends on the relationship between declarations and behavior, explain that measurement boundary where readers need it.

## Preserve a qualification that changes the claim

**Source:**

> Policy adoption is associated with higher sales. Firms may adopt the policy when they expect demand to grow, so the comparison does not establish a causal effect.

**Meaning-changing edit:**

> Policy adoption raises sales.

**Faithful revision:**

> Firms adopting the policy have higher sales. They may adopt it when they expect demand to grow. This selection limits a causal interpretation of the comparison.

The source names a concrete identification problem. That qualification earns its place because it changes how readers can interpret the result. In later passages, "is associated with" can carry the established scope without repeating the full explanation.

## Keep a passage that already works

> The sample covers purchases at one retailer. Within this sample, repeat buyers spend more per visit than first-time buyers.

Each sentence carries one message, and the second uses the scope established by the first. Keep this passage when it fits the surrounding argument. Adding a signpost or a list of unobserved markets would add no understanding.
